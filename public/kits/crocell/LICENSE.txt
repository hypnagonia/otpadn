Sampled and engineered for DrumGizmo by Lars Muldjord (drumgizmo.org).
Drumkit setup: Andreas / Crocell (crocell.dk). Mic setup: Jacob Olsen, JBOSound (jbosound.com).

Changes made for Otpadn: a subset of hits (8 velocity layers per piece, one kick, snare, three toms,
hi-hat, crash, ride) was selected; the 15 microphone channels were summed into 7 tracks
(kick, snare, toms, hihat, ride, overheads, room); levels scaled by one global gain; the toms track keeps only tom hits (no bleed); tails trimmed;
encoded as AAC. Build script: tools/build_crocell_kit.py.
