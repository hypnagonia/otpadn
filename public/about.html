Otpadn — a free DAW that runs in your browser

Otpadn takes a song from idea to mix without installing anything: split it into stems, turn parts into MIDI, write harmonies, generate new parts and mix it with automation and pro plug-ins. Everything runs on your own computer — your audio is never uploaded.

Open the DAW

From a finished song to editable parts

AI stem splitter and vocal remover

Split any song into drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and other with HTDemucs running on your GPU, or use the fast DSP split. Mute the vocals for a karaoke or practice track, or solo the bass to learn it.

Audio to MIDI

Transcribe vocals, bass, guitar, keys and drums into MIDI and edit them in the piano roll or as guitar tablature.

Dereverb and denoise

Clean up a recording or a stem in one pass with a neural dereverb / denoise model.

Write and arrange

Chord-aware harmony writer

Add harmonies to a vocal or lead line: singable, in key, following the song's chords — 3rds, 6ths, stacks above and below.

Part generator

Generate a bass guitar line locked to the kick drum, voice-led pads, arpeggios, acoustic strums or double-tracked metal riffs in a drop tuning — all following the song's chords and sections. Bass lines get natural slides into notes and finger falls before rests.

Drum and part producers, auto-arrange

Clean up and rework drum and instrument MIDI, and build a section-aware arrangement automatically.

Mix like a record

  • A real multitrack acoustic drum kit with separate kick, snare, tom, hi-hat, ride, overhead and room channels, sample layers and a modern metal mix.
  • Inserts, sends, buses, sidechain compression, automation with latch write, clip fades and crossfades, track freeze.
  • Compressor, multiband, delay, plate reverb, saturator, limiter, match EQ, transient designer, amp sim, and ports of the Airwindows ButterComp2, Density2, Galactic and ClipOnly2.
  • Count-in recording, metronome, WAV and stem bounce, MIDI export, save and open project files.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — no account, no install.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. All processing happens in your browser; sessions are saved locally and can be saved to disk as project files.

Which browsers work?

Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari work best (AI features use WebGPU). Firefox runs the DAW; AI stem splitting falls back to the CPU.
