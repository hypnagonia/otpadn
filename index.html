Otpadn — a free DAW in your browser

Drop a song, split it into stems with AI, turn any part into MIDI, write harmonies, generate new parts and mix it like a record — without installing anything. Your audio is processed on your own computer and never uploaded.

What you can do

  • AI stem splitter — drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and other (HTDemucs, on your GPU), plus a fast DSP split.
  • Audio to MIDI — transcribe vocals, bass, guitar, keys and drums into editable MIDI.
  • Harmony writer — chord-aware, singable 3rds, 6ths and stacks for vocal and lead lines.
  • Part generator — bass guitar, pads, arpeggios, acoustic strums and double-tracked metal riffs that follow the song's chords and kick drum.
  • Multitrack drum kit — a real acoustic kit with separate mic channels, sample layers and a modern metal mix.
  • Pro mixing — inserts, sends, sidechain, automation, clip fades, freeze, Airwindows plug-ins, match EQ, transient designer.
  • Record, arrange and export — count-in recording, piano roll and tablature, auto-arrange, WAV and MIDI export, project files.
